Senem Işık

Hi! I’m Senem Işık, a PhD student at Stanford University advised by Amin Saberi.

I am broadly interested in the foundations of learning and decision-making systems, often motivated by social and economic contexts. At Stanford, I have been affiliated with the Operations Research and Theory groups, the Center for Computational Market Design, the Language, Data, and Reasoning Lab, the SOAL (Society & Algorithms) Lab, and the Stanford AI Lab. In the summer of 2026, I interned as an Applied Scientist at Amazon (AWS) Science, hosted by Nina Mishra.

Previously, I completed my Bachelor’s with Honors in Mathematics and Master’s in Computer Science also at Stanford :) where I was advised by Tselil Schramm and Jinyoung Park. I am originally from Istanbul, Türkiye. I pronounce my full name as Senæm ɯˈʃɯk, and here are three places that are close to my heart.
