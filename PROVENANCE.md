# What this package is

This is a **new, portable reconstruction**, not the original source repository or a byte-for-byte export of the hosted website. Retrieval of the source at https://senem-isik.senem-x1.chatgpt.site/ failed in this session, and the earlier Sites source/export actions were not available. The original deployment and Git history are not included. No publication or account change was performed when producing these files.

## Materials used

- The saved desktop-top, desktop-bottom, and mobile-top previews supplied in the conversation.
- The original uploaded photograph (`photo.png`, 898 × 1282 pixels), included unchanged.
- The owner's Google Site for text and link destinations: https://sites.google.com/view/senem-ashak/home
- The subsequent content, layout, typography, color, and removal instructions in the conversation.

The beige, body text, black titles, muted blue, green, orange, and divider colors were sampled from the saved previews. The responsive layout was rebuilt rather than copied from inaccessible CSS. The flower is a recreated SVG based on the saved preview; it is used consistently for the tab and Expository toggle. Exact typography can also vary with the serif fonts installed on the viewer's computer. No font files are included.

## Items to review before treating this as your definitive copy

1. **Uma Dayal's hyperlink:** the original Google Site supplied `https://uma-dayal/`, which is not a usable public destination. The name remains in the author list, as plain text. Add the intended URL in `index.html` once known.
2. **The CURIS venue hyperlink:** the original Google Site linked the CURIS venue to the same JMM 2025 page used for a different paper. The CURIS venue remains present and italicized, but deliberately has no link rather than pointing to the unrelated event.
3. **Older external destinations:** some exact paths could not be recovered, so the three-places links and DIMACS use the relevant institutional homepages where necessary. Paper, poster, slide, and thesis URLs that were recoverable were retained. Linked Google Drive documents and external websites were not downloaded into this package, and their public permissions and ongoing availability were not comprehensively verified.
4. **Publication/news status:** these reflect the content and edits provided in the conversation, not an independent update of research outcomes. No new August news item was invented. Genesis Therapeutics and Jamcoders remain removed.
5. **Exact visual identity:** compare this reconstructed page with the currently hosted page before replacing any existing site. The exported photo is exact; the HTML, styling, layout, and flower are reconstructed.
